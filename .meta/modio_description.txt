<p>Replaces the default beaver selection highlight with a golden vertical beam and ground circle. The highlight smoothly follows the selected beaver using the animated model position.</p>
<h3 id="features">Features</h3>
<ul>
<li>Golden vertical beam rising above the selected beaver</li>
<li>Golden ground circle at the beaver's feet</li>
<li>Smooth tracking via CharacterModel.Position</li>
<li>Suppresses the vanilla selection highlight for beavers</li>
</ul>
