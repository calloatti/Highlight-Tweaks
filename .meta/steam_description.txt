Replaces the default beaver selection highlight with a golden vertical beam and ground circle. The highlight smoothly follows the selected beaver using the animated model position.

[h3]Features[/h3]
[list]
[*] Golden vertical beam rising above the selected beaver
[*] Golden ground circle at the beaver's feet
[*] Smooth tracking via CharacterModel.Position
[*] Suppresses the vanilla selection highlight for beavers
[/list]
